# Cosecha Business Plan Framing

Session learning: when Lorenzo asks for business-plan or company-philosophy work around sourcing/distribution, frame it as **Cosecha**, not Selva Partners generically.

## Hierarchy

```text
Selva Partners
= broader company / holding vision / operating platform

Cosecha
= sourcing, distribution, chef-direct, wholesale, hospitality, cruise, produce intelligence, and market execution

Cosecha Intel
= market/product/pricing/buyer/supplier intelligence layer supporting Cosecha

Selva Intelligence OS / Agent System
= Notion + Google Workspace + operating agents that help run Selva/Cosecha
```

## Business-plan posture

Use a Zero-to-One / first-principles style: detailed, opinionated, strategic, and concrete. Do not write generic distributor or MBA-plan language.

Core framing:

> Cosecha is the sourcing and distribution arm of Selva Partners, built to connect exceptional growers and producers with high-taste buyers through trusted relationships, market intelligence, and agent-powered execution.

Sharper commercial framing:

> Cosecha helps chefs, hotels, cruise buyers, and specialty wholesale accounts know what is worth buying, when to buy it, and who to trust behind it.

Zero-to-One framing:

> Cosecha believes the future of specialty food distribution belongs not to the largest catalog, but to the most trusted intelligence layer between producers and buyers.

## Connect to the broader Selva blueprint

Cosecha planning should reflect the broader Selva company blueprint, especially:

1. **Selva Trucks** — logistics, lane economics, route density, freshness, delivery reliability, truck margins, freight opportunities.
2. **Cosecha Chef-Direct** — Michelin/JB, chef-driven restaurants, premium hospitality, sampling, seasonal drops, relationship capture, repeat orders.
3. **FullFilled / B2B synergy** — relevant learnings from family drops, wellness menus, luxury hospitality, company sampling, and recurring food programs, while keeping Cosecha distinct as sourcing/distribution.
4. **Cosecha Intel** — market reads, product windows, pricing movement, shortage alerts, supplier opportunities, competitor movement, restaurant/hotel/cruise signals. Must feed sales actions, not just reports.

## Value system to embed

- Product must have identity: variety + grower/farm + origin + growing method + harvest window + use case + buyer fit.
- Cosecha sells judgment, not inventory lists.
- Trust is economic margin; every operational failure can erode margin.
- Intelligence is part of the product, not content marketing.
- Start with a narrow monopoly: high-taste South Florida hospitality buyers before broad expansion.
- Avoid anonymous commodity claims, fake scarcity, and unverified product stories.
- Public/newsletter copy remains internal until Lorenzo approves; use verified grower/farm names and do not expose raw Natoora archive data.
