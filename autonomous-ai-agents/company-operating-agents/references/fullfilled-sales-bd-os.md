# FullFilled Foods Sales/BD Operating OS

Session-specific reference for applying the company operating-agent blueprint to FullFilled Foods.

## Commercial context

- Lorenzo is retained at $2,300/month for ~20–21 hours/month.
- He earns 7% of sales he brings in.
- The operating system should optimize for selling actions, not analysis volume.
- Treat the business as a Sales + BD engine with a lightweight CRM and weekly action loop.

## Core positioning

FullFilled Foods should be positioned as a quality-first, end-to-end culinary partner, not generic catering or commodity meal prep.

Preferred language:

> FullFilled creates quality-first food programs for families, companies, private clients, and curated experiences — from weekly Tuesday Drops to meetings, events, wellness menus, and full end-to-end catering.

Natoora-style presentation flow to reuse:

```text
Mission
→ why food quality matters
→ sourcing philosophy
→ product/food beauty
→ operational reliability
→ customer experience
→ specific program
→ taste/sample/pilot
```

## Offer examples

### Family / DTC: Tuesday Drop

- Once weekly, Tuesday drop.
- 3 meal sets.
- Example pricing: $600/week for 3 people; $750/week for 4 people.
- Not fully à la carte; chef-led menu protects quality and operational consistency.
- Dietary restrictions/preferences are accommodated.
- Quality is non-negotiable; organic/sustainable sourcing whenever possible.
- FullFilled can handle purchasing, packaging, delivery, serviceware, and the broader food program end-to-end.
- This is only one family example; FullFilled can do broader food programs.

Positioning line:

> Private-chef quality, weekly-drop convenience.

### Companies / offices

- Meetings, sampling, wellness lunches, recurring food programs.
- Sales path: direct outreach → sample drop → meeting → 2-week pilot → recurring program.
- Companies should be sold through meetings and sampling; tasting is the close.

### White-label wellness

Targets: gyms, Pilates/yoga studios, med spas, wellness clinics, luxury residential buildings, private clubs, creators/nutritionists.

Offer:

> Your weekly wellness menu, powered by FullFilled.

### Luxury hospitality/private clients

Targets: private jets/FBOs, yacht charters, yacht provisioning, villa managers, concierges, private clubs, hotels/residences.

Offer:

> High-touch culinary fulfillment for VIP guests, private residences, yachts, aviation, events, and last-minute hospitality needs.

## Marketing split

- Families/DTC: Instagram and TikTok are preferred top-of-funnel channels.
- Companies/partners: meetings and sample drops are preferred conversion channels.

Family CTA:

> DM “TUESDAY” for the next family drop.

## 20-hour monthly plan

Use the Selva Sales Wing operating principle:

> Do not optimize for agent output. Optimize for selling actions.

Every hour should produce at least one:

1. Person/company to contact
2. Message to send
3. Meeting to book
4. Sample drop to arrange
5. Family/household lead to convert
6. Stale opportunity to follow up
7. Next action logged

Recommended monthly allocation:

| Workstream | Hours/month |
|---|---:|
| Sales command / pipeline planning | 3 |
| Lead research + enrichment | 4 |
| Outreach + follow-up drafting | 4 |
| Sampling / meetings coordination | 5 |
| Instagram/TikTok family funnel | 2 |
| CRM capture + reporting | 2 |

Monthly minimum KPIs:

- 40 new leads researched
- 80 outreach touches
- 20 follow-ups
- 4–6 meetings or serious conversations
- 3–5 sample drops
- 2 pilot proposals
- 1–2 closed recurring customers/partners
