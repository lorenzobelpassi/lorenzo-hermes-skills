## Sales OS Interpretation

For FullFilled Foods, run three revenue loops in parallel:

```text
B2B corporate/institutional sales
+ BD partnerships / food-startup / CPG support
+ Direct-to-consumer meal, wellness, and private-event offers
```

The highest priority should be recurring revenue opportunities because they compound Lorenzo’s 7% upside:
- Corporate meal/snack programs
- Institutional food programs
- Weekly wellness meal plans
- Ongoing food startup / CPG R&D retainers

## Lead Scoring Priorities

Rank leads by:
1. Recurring monthly revenue potential
2. Speed to meeting / sample / proposal
3. Likelihood to close
4. Fit with FullFilled service lines
5. Relationship or warm-intro strength
6. Commission upside to Lorenzo
7. Repeat/referral potential

## High-Value Target Segments

- Startups / tech offices needing food programs, office snacks, wellness lunches, team events
- Corporate firms needing recurring meals, fundraisers, executive events, employee wellness
- Institutions and nonprofits needing food service or events
- Food/beverage startups needing costing, packaging, SOPs, launch support
- CPG brands needing R&D, shelf-life, tasting, sampling, or event activation
- DTC buyers for weekly wellness plans, private dining, neighborhood drops, and curated experiences
