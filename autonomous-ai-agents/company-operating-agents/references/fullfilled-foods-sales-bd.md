# FullFilled Foods Sales + BD Operating Notes

Session-derived blueprint for applying the company operating-agent pattern to FullFilled Foods.

## Business context

- Lorenzo is retained for FullFilled Foods at about $2.3k/month for ~20–21 hours/month.
- He earns 7% of sales he brings in, so the operating system should optimize for revenue-producing activity, not general admin.
- Core positioning: quality-first, end-to-end culinary programs for families, companies, private clients, and curated experiences.
- Quality language matters: organic and sustainable whenever possible; dietary restrictions/preferences handled carefully.
- FullFilled can manage the food program end-to-end, including purchasing, logistics, packaging, and serviceware.

## Sales channels to run in parallel

1. **Families / DTC**
   - Primary channels: Instagram and TikTok.
   - Example offer: Tuesday family wellness drop.
   - Marketing style: warm, visual, lifestyle, family relief, trust, quality, sourcing, behind-the-scenes.

2. **Companies / BD**
   - Primary conversion path: meeting -> sample drop -> pilot -> recurring program.
   - Targets: offices, corporate wellness, white-label partners, buildings, clinics, gyms, private clubs, hospitality teams.

3. **Luxury / private hospitality**
   - Targets: private jets/FBOs, yacht charters/provisioning, private villas, luxury residences, concierge companies, hotels/clubs.
   - Position as VIP culinary fulfillment, not generic catering.

## Example family offer: Tuesday Drop

- Once weekly, Tuesday drop.
- 3 meal sets.
- $600/week for 3 people.
- $750/week for 4 people.
- Clients do not choose à la carte; menu is chef-led to protect quality and consistency.
- Dietary restrictions/preferences are accommodated.
- Positioning: private-chef quality, weekly-drop convenience.
- This is an example for families, not the whole business.

## White-label weekly wellness menu

Use for partners with existing audiences:
- gyms, pilates/yoga studios, wellness clinics, med spas, nutritionists, luxury residential buildings, private clubs, concierges, corporate offices, influencers/creators.

Pilot structure:
- once-weekly menu/drop
- pre-orders only
- limited choices
- dietary notes handled
- partner can co-brand, fully white-label, or refer for a commission

## Natoora presentation flow adaptation

Base FullFilled pitch around mission before offer details:

1. Mission: food should make people feel cared for at home, work, in travel, and during special experiences.
2. Problem: families/companies want high-quality food but do not want to manage planning, sourcing, cooking, logistics, dietary needs, or serviceware.
3. Solution: FullFilled handles the food program end-to-end.
4. Quality philosophy: organic/sustainable/seasonal whenever possible; quality is non-negotiable.
5. Offer examples: Tuesday Drop, weekly wellness menus, meetings, events, white-label programs, VIP travel/hospitality.
6. Proof: taste first; sampling is the main sales tool for companies.

## Pitfalls

- Do not describe the Tuesday Drop as the entire company; it is one sellable family example.
- Do not over-customize the menu in positioning. Emphasize chef-led menus plus dietary care.
- Do not sell companies mostly through social content; use meetings and sampling.
- Do not call premium household service just “meal prep.” Position between private chef and meal delivery.
- Do not expose raw/private Natoora archive data in marketing. Use the mission/presentation flow and relationship learnings, not confidential material.
