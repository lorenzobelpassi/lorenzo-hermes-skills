# Palatable Notion cockpit for revenue operating systems

Use this when building Notion around a sales/revenue plan with many backend databases or agents. The key design rule is: backend can be complex; daily UI must stay calm and action-led.

## Front-page principle

The daily Notion page should answer only:

- What do I do today?
- Who do I need to see/call?
- What am I selling or sampling?
- What needs approval?
- What did I learn?
- What happens next?

Do not show every database on the daily front page. Every visible row should imply an action.

## Recommended front-page sections

Limit to about six sections:

1. Today — due/overdue/top money-moving actions; max ~5 visible rows.
2. People To See — route-ready accounts only; max ~10 rows.
3. Product/Sample Angle — 3-6 relevant items, not the full catalog.
4. Drafts To Approve — ready-to-send communication; no raw unverified lead emails.
5. Capture — quick buttons/templates/voice-note intake for field updates.
6. End of Day — won/lost/moved deals, follow-ups created, product feedback, tomorrow's must-dos.

## Backend databases may be rich, but hidden

For a revenue OS, backend databases can include Accounts, Contacts, Opportunities, Interactions, Market Signals, Product Catalog, Sample Kits, and Source Documents. Keep full tables, raw imports, and document archives behind filtered linked views or backend pages.

## Six-month plan scoreboard

Add a compact scoreboard near the top:

- Target net by end date.
- Days elapsed / days remaining.
- Percent through plan.
- Required monthly pace.
- Gap-to-target / remaining days once actual booked net is loaded.

This should motivate action, not become a complex analytics dashboard.

## Pitfalls

- Do not import raw archives directly into the user-facing cockpit.
- Do not make the user manage eight databases manually every morning.
- Do not show raw Source Documents, full Product Catalog, full Contacts, or raw Market Signals on the front page.
- Do not let automation create visual clutter; agents should update backend rows and surface only the next action.
