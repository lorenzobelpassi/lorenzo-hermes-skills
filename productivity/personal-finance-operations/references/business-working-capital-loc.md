# Business Working-Capital LOC Research Notes

Use when Lorenzo asks for a line of credit or short-cycle financing for Selva/Cosecha/Bey Bey produce float.

- Buyer payment terms: ~30 days
- Supplier payment terms: ~15 days

- Working capital required: $30,000
- Base weekly volume: 800 cases/week

- Gross revenue/load: ~$49,400

Recommended target line: **$75,000 revolving LOC**. Use $30k as the modeled core need, with cushion for timing variability, late payers, rejected/damaged product, overlapping orders, and growth.

## Lender positioning

Do **not** frame as “I need money for Bey Bey.” Frame as receivables/payables timing:

> We are seeking a $75,000 revolving working-capital line to support short-cycle produce distribution. Our model shows a 15-day cash conversion gap: suppliers are generally paid in approximately 15 days while buyers pay in approximately 30 days. At base volume, the business requires approximately $30,000 in float. The requested line provides cushion for timing variability, overlapping purchase cycles, and confirmed buyer receivables. Draws will be tied to product movement and repaid from customer collections.

Preferred terminology: “working-capital line,” “confirmed product movement,” “payables/receivables bridge,” “cash conversion gap,” “receivables repayment.”

## LOC option ranking discovered May 2026

### 1. Bank of America Business Advantage Credit Line
Best if Lorenzo qualifies; true bank LOC, cleanest terms.

- Unsecured, no collateral required
- Line amount from $10k
- Purpose language explicitly includes bridging payables/receivables and seasonal inventory
- Prime + 0% intro variable rate for first 7 billing cycles
- $0 origination fee
- $150 annual fee, waived first year
- Typical minimums shown: personal credit above 700 FICO, 2 years in business, $100k annual revenue

### 2. American Express Business Line of Credit
Best fast institutional option, especially with existing Amex relationship.

- Line sizes $2k–$250k; initial lines above $150k only select borrowers
- Minimums shown: 1+ year in business, FICO 660+, recent average monthly revenue $3k+
- Must link business bank account
- All loans secured by business assets; every loan requires personal guarantee
- Can disburse to business bank account or directly to US vendor/payee
- 1-month single repayment fee: 0.95%–1.80%; 2-month 1.90%–3.75%; 3-month 2.85%–6.05%
- 6-month installment fee 3%–9%; 12-month 6%–18%; 18-month 9%–27%; 24-month 12%–18%

For a $30k 1-month draw, fee range at 0.95%–1.80% = ~$285–$540. Good if used surgically for confirmed-margin produce deals.

### 3. Bluevine Line of Credit
Best fintech backup.

- Lines up to $250k
- Advertised rates as low as 7.8% for top qualifying customers
- No maintenance fees
- Decision as fast as 5 minutes; draws as fast as 24 hours or instant to Bluevine Business Checking
- Reports repayment history to Experian business credit
- Issued by Celtic Bank, serviced by Bluevine
- Personal guarantee/default personal reporting risk disclosed

A $30k draw for 15 days at 7.8% APR is ~$96 interest, but approved pricing may be higher.

### 4. Fundbox
Fallback if entity is young.

- Up to $250k funding
- Requirements shown: 3+ months in business, $30k+ annual revenue, business checking account
- Likely easier and more expensive than bank options
- Consider asking $50k first unless revenue supports $75k

### 5. OnDeck
Emergency only due to expensive average pricing.

- Lines $6k–$200k
- Terms 12/18/24 months; weekly or monthly payments
- Requirements shown: 1 year in business, $100k annual revenue, 625 FICO, business checking
- Disclosed average LOC APR: 56.6% for originations in half-year ending June 30, 2025
- $30k draw for 15 days at 56.6% APR is ~$698 interest

## Application order

1. Check BofA eligibility / prequal if 2-year history and $100k+ revenue are plausible.
2. Check Amex pre-approval if Lorenzo has Amex relationship.
3. Apply Bluevine if bank path is too slow or uncertain.
4. Keep Fundbox as young-entity fallback.
5. Avoid OnDeck unless a specific order’s margin clearly absorbs the high capital cost.

## Pitfalls

- Do not apply everywhere randomly; hard pulls and signals can hurt underwriting.
- Do not size the ask exactly to modeled need. Ask for cushion: $75k line, but plan to use $30k–$45k at a time unless overlapping confirmed orders exist.
- Distinguish APR interest from fixed draw fees; a short 15-day produce cycle can make fixed loan fees proportionally expensive.
- Identify PG, UCC/blanket lien, annual fees, origination fees, minimum draws, repayment frequency, and whether early payoff reduces fees before recommending acceptance.
