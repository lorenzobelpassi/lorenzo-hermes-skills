## Reader-first design correction

Lorenzo explicitly wants The Walk-In to be a **great reading experience for the reader**. Do not over-index on making the page dark/cool if it hurts readability.

Operating rule:
- The brand can be sensual/hot; the article reading experience should be smooth.
- Use dark/wine/tomato/RLP 305 identity for the wrapper: avatar, masthead, covers, images, dividers.
- Keep post pages calm and highly readable: strong contrast, comfortable body size, generous line height, short paragraphs, clean section breaks.
- Prefer title case for long post titles; avoid all caps for long reading titles.
- Use all caps only for small labels/kickers.
- Use 1–3 intentional images per post; images should enrich the read, not interrupt it.
- If Substack forces compromises, prioritize in this order: readable body, RLP 305 avatar, The Walk-In masthead, tomato accent, clean nav/sections, sensual imagery, fancy backgrounds/colors.
