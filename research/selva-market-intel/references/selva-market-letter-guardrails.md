# Selva Market Letter Guardrails

Session learning: Lorenzo wants the Market Intel / Lead Gen output to become a weekly branded newsletter, not immediate outbound sales.

## Hard guardrails

- Internal intelligence first; no third-party sends unless Lorenzo explicitly approves.
- No automated Chloe outreach, Gmail sends, DMs, WhatsApp/text, or LinkedIn messages from scraped leads.
- Pipeline rows generated from Restaurant Intel should default to Needs Review / Nurture until Lorenzo approves contact/list/send.
- Natoora 2025 sales archive is internal intelligence only. Do not expose competitor quantities, account names, or wording like “Natoora sales show…” in public-facing copy.
- Public copy can use generalized seasonality, verified availability, grower stories, market observations, and Selva’s own view.

## Grower universe correction

Lorenzo corrected the sourcing rule: newsletter growers should be the growers on the **INBOUND Sheet**, not random organic/minimal-intervention farms found online.

Use this hierarchy:
1. INBOUND Sheet growers = source of truth for grower/farm names.
2. Within INBOUND, prefer organic, certified organic, regenerative, biodynamic, no-spray, low-input, or minimal-intervention growers.
3. Do not feature anonymous commodity product in the newsletter.
4. Do not add a new grower to the newsletter unless Lorenzo explicitly approves it.

If the INBOUND Sheet is missing/unavailable, ask Lorenzo for the exact Google Sheet link/name instead of inventing grower names.

## Weekly newsletter workflow

1. Read INBOUND Sheet: grower/farm, product lines, organic/minimal standard, region, availability, relationship notes.
2. Cross-reference with seasonality / Natoora 2025 archive internally to identify product windows.
3. Select featured items only when farm/grower + product + current availability are verifiable.
4. Draft subscriber-facing Selva Market Letter.
5. Keep an internal-only section with source basis, competitor/archive signal, target accounts, and approval status.
6. Send nothing until Lorenzo approves final copy and recipient list.

## Preferred newsletter shape

Working title: Selva Market Letter.

Sections:
- This Week’s Market Read
- Product Window / What Is Coming In
- Grower/Farm Note
- Chef Note / Bar Note
- Miami Watch Items
- Availability / Samples
- Quiet CTA

Tone: direct, understated, useful, chef-facing. Avoid “premium,” “best-in-class,” “exclusive,” and generic luxury language.

## Citrus example direction

Do not write generic “citrus.” Use actual varieties when verified: Cara Cara, Meyer lemon, Oro Blanco grapefruit, Nagami kumquat, Gold Nugget tangerine, finger lime, Makrut lime leaf, yuzu, Seville orange, Sorrento lemon.

Public-facing item format:
- Variety
- INBOUND farm/grower name
- Growing standard
- Origin
- Current window / availability
- Plate/bar use

Example skeleton:

**Cara Cara Orange — [INBOUND Farm], [standard], [origin]**
Lower acid than standard navel, with deeper color and a berry-like note. Good for crudo, bitter greens, seafood, desserts, and cocktails.
