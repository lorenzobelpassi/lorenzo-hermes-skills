# FullFilled Foods Outreach Notes

Use when drafting or executing FullFilled Foods sales/BD outreach for Lorenzo.

## Positioning

FullFilled is not just catering or meal prep. Frame it as **quality-first, end-to-end culinary programs** for families, companies, private clients, hospitality, and curated experiences.

Use the Natoora-style presentation flow:

```text
Mission → why food quality matters → sourcing philosophy → product/food beauty → operational reliability → customer experience → specific program → next step
```

Core language:
- Quality is non-negotiable.
- Organic/sustainable whenever possible.
- Dietary restrictions/preferences are handled.
- FullFilled can manage the full food program: menu, purchasing, prep, packaging, delivery/drop, dietary notes, and serviceware when needed.

## Example family offer

The family offer is an example, not the whole company:
- Once weekly Tuesday drop
- 3 meal sets
- $600/week for 3 people
- $750/week for 4 people
- Limited choice to protect quality/operations, but dietary restrictions and preferences are handled

Suggested positioning:

> Private-chef quality, weekly-drop convenience.

## Channel split

Families / DTC:
- Instagram + TikTok are primary top-of-funnel.
- Content should show Tuesday Drop, sourcing, prep, packaging, family relief, dietary care, and quality.
- CTA examples: `DM TUESDAY`, `small number of Tuesday spots open`.

Companies / BD:
- Sell through direct outreach, meetings, and sampling.
- Path: lead → outreach → sample → meeting → pilot → recurring program.
- Do not over-explain; get them to taste.

White-label wellness:
- Target gyms, pilates/yoga studios, med spas, wellness clinics, residential buildings, clubs, concierges, influencers/nutritionists.
- Frame as `[Partner] weekly wellness menu, powered by FullFilled`.

Luxury/private hospitality:
- Target private aviation, yachts, villa managers, estate managers, concierges, private clubs, luxury hotels/residences.
- Frame as VIP culinary fulfillment / end-to-end food support.
