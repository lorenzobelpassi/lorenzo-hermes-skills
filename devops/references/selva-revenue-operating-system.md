# Selva Revenue Operating System — May 2026 Rethink

Use this when Lorenzo asks about the six-month Selva plan, infrastructure, lead research, market intel, CRM, or pipeline discipline.

## Key framing

Do not frame the setup as “agents that run.” Frame it as a revenue operating system:

1. Market Intelligence finds opportunities.
2. Lead Research turns opportunities into named accounts + people.
3. CRM stores every target, conversation, quote, sample, and next step.
4. Pipeline Tracker forces daily follow-up.
5. Deal Log captures calls/voice/emails and keeps the CRM alive.
6. Weekly review measures progress against the May–Oct 2026 $157K net target.

The agents are workers; Notion/CRM is the operating system.

## Recommended Notion CRM structure

### 1. Accounts
Restaurants, hotels, cruise lines, wholesalers, distributors, farms, freight contacts.

Suggested fields:
- Account Name
- Type: Restaurant / Hotel / Cruise / Wholesaler / Distributor / Farm / Freight / Other
- Tier: A / B / C / D
- Zone: Brickell, Miami Beach, Alapattah, etc.
- Segment: Michelin, Natoora target, cruise, luxury hotel, wholesaler, chef-direct
- Current Supplier: Natoora / Baldor / FreshPoint / unknown
- Estimated Produce Spend
- Selva Fit Score
- Status: Researching / Ready for Outreach / Contacted / Warm / Sampling / Quoting / Active / Dead
- Owner: Lorenzo / Chloe / Mariano
- Next Action Date
- Last Touch Date
- Source
- Notes

### 2. Contacts
People attached to accounts.

Suggested fields:
- Name
- Role: Chef / Owner / GM / Buyer / Purchasing / F&B Director
- Email
- Phone
- Instagram
- LinkedIn
- Account relation
- Confidence: Verified / Probable / Unverified
- Preferred Channel
- Notes

### 3. Opportunities
Potential revenue and margin.

Suggested fields:
- Opportunity Name
- Account relation
- Commodity
- Volume
- Gross Revenue Estimate
- Net Margin Estimate
- Stage: Lead / Contacted / Interested / Sample Sent / Quoted / Negotiating / Won / Lost
- Close Probability
- Expected Close Date
- Next Action
- Next Action Date
- Loss Reason

### 4. Interactions
Every email, call, voice note, meeting, sample, quote.

Suggested fields:
- Date
- Account
- Contact
- Channel
- Summary
- Sentiment
- Next Step
- Source: Gmail / Plaud / Manual / Discord / Market Intel

### 5. Market Signals
Actionable intelligence from the Market Intel Agent.

Suggested fields:
- Signal
- Commodity
- Source
- Urgency
- Implication
- Suggested Account Targets
- Action Taken?
- Converted to Lead?

## Pipeline stages

Use strict stages so the Pipeline Tracker can act:

0. Raw Lead
1. Qualified
2. Contact Identified
3. Ready for Outreach
4. Contacted
5. Engaged
6. Sample / Meeting
7. Quoted
8. Negotiating
9. Won
10. Active Account
11. Lost / Nurture

Every live record should have a Next Action, Next Action Date, Owner, Stage, Last Touch, and reason for being in that stage. No record should sit without a next action.

## Lead scoring model

Selva Fit Score, 0–100:

- 30 pts: Buyer quality — Michelin, James Beard, chef-driven, luxury hotel, cruise, serious F&B operation
- 20 pts: Product fit — seasonal, organic, specialty, quality-sensitive produce buyer
- 15 pts: Geography — Miami priority zones, Palm Beach luxury, Caribbean logistics fit
- 15 pts: Buying power — high menu price, volume, multi-location, hotel/cruise scale
- 10 pts: Timing trigger — opening, new chef, menu change, event, supplier issue, recent press
- 10 pts: Contactability — verified email/phone/IG/LinkedIn or known path

Tiers:
- 85–100: A+ target. Lorenzo touches personally.
- 70–84: A target. Chloe enriches/researches; Lorenzo sends.
- 55–69: B target. Batch outreach or nurture.
- Below 55: ignore unless there is a specific trigger.

Lead lanes:
1. Natoora takeover targets
2. Michelin / James Beard / fine dining
3. Luxury hotels and resorts
4. Cruise lines / provisioning
5. Alapattah wholesalers
6. Caribbean buyers
7. New openings

Each lane needs a different pitch, cadence, required data fields, and owner.

## Daily command output

Instead of separate disconnected agent messages, aim for one daily Selva command brief:

1. Market conditions
2. Best trade/opportunity signals today
3. Top 10 accounts to touch
4. Follow-ups due
5. Stalled deals
6. New leads found
7. Pipeline vs $157K target
8. Agent health: what ran, what failed, what was created, what needs review

## Weekly operating cadence

Daily 5am: market brief + trade ideas + follow-ups due.
Daily 8am: Lorenzo acts on top 10 selling actions.
End of day: Deal Log captures calls/voice notes and updates CRM.
Monday: Lead Research adds 25–50 qualified leads.
Friday: Pipeline review — leads added, contacts touched, replies, samples, quotes, won deals, expected net margin, progress vs $157K.

North star: qualified selling actions and net pipeline movement, not number of agents run.
